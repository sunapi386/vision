title: "Part VI: What Comes Next"
---

The previous five parts described the growth of agent work, gaps in accountability, useful historical patterns, a proposed architecture, and the transitions needed to put it to use. This part explores what that infrastructure could make possible.

The examples are scenarios, with assumptions and limits. They lead from changes in professional work to choices about ownership and control, and finally to the human purposes the infrastructure should serve. Capability creates possibilities; institutions and people determine how those possibilities are used.
