title: "Part VI: What Comes Next"
---

Picture an ordinary workday a few years from now. A supervising partner reviews only the contract clauses the system flagged, while the junior associate tunes the rules that do the flagging. A police officer is back on patrol thirty-five minutes sooner after a call. An office manager in southern Ontario earns a fee each time another small manufacturer deploys the invoice workflow she built.

This part is about that day: what work looks like when the accountability stack exists, who owns the machines underneath it, and what the whole thing is for.

The examples are scenarios built on stated assumptions. Some of what they describe runs today; much of it is a design I am arguing for, and the full system does not exist yet. I say that once here. The detailed limits are collected under Scope and limits, in the Verification section at the back of the book.

Capability creates the options. People and institutions decide which ones get used.
