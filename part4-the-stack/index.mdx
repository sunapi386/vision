# Part IV: What the Stack Requires

If the pattern holds, if the agent economy must produce an accountability layer, then what does that layer actually look like? Seven layers, not one protocol, each building on the one below: accountability, compute, exchange, trust, enforcement, agency, and marketplace. Together, they form the complete infrastructure for tracking costs, tracing conclusions, governing data, allocating resources, and settling transactions across organizational boundaries.

The stack rests on a foundation: sovereignty. The accountability guarantees mean nothing if the infrastructure is controlled by a third party. And the stack must be an open protocol, not a proprietary platform, because the point is interoperability across every boundary.
